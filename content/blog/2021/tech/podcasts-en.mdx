### What is a podcast?
+ In the narrow sense, a podcast usually means an independent podcast following the production process described above. Distribution is decentralized, and all content is under your control. You therefore need your own hosting server, domain, and RSS subscription URL containing that domain. Even if the hosting server goes down, listeners can still find you through the fixed RSS link.
+ A podcast is digital media: a series of audio, video, electronic radio, or text files published online as a list. Listeners subscribe through electronic devices to download or stream the files. “Podcast” blends “iPod” and “broadcast”.
+ Simply put, a podcast is an audio program. It usually contains original audio or video recordings, but can also distribute recordings of TV or radio shows, talks, performances, or other events. It is entirely free content created around the world.
+ In 2005, Apple CEO Steve Jobs said: “Podcasting is the next generation of radio. Users can now subscribe to over 3,000 free Podcasts and have each new episode automatically delivered over the Internet to their computers and iPods.” By 2020, the iTunes Store offered over a million podcast episodes, with over 750,000 independent podcasts worldwide. Distribution had expanded beyond computers and iPods to phones, smartwatches, smart speakers (Alexa, HomePod), CarPlay, and RSS subscriptions.
