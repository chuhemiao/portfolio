Economist Robert Shiller made a well-known argument in _Irrational Exuberance_: stock prices are driven by sentiment. But the sentiment that pushes a stock market higher does not always have to be optimism. Under certain circumstances, fear and pessimism can also drive prices up. The resulting stock-market boom might be better described as a “tragic boom.”
