1.Download the extension package, extract it, and enter its directory.

2.Find`phpize`directory, then run`/usr/local/php/bin/phpize`

3. `./configure --with-php-config=/usr/local/php/bin/php-config`

4.`--with-php-config=/usr/local/php/bin/php-config`specifies the installation path here.

5.If you do not know the`phpize`directory, run`whereis phpize`or`find / -name phpize`
