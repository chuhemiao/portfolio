## Two ways to create headings in Markdown
+ First: add “=” or “-” below the text for level-one or level-two headings, respectively.
+ Second: prefix the text with “#”; its count determines the heading level. There are levels 1–6, with level 1 the largest.
## Text emphasis
+ *Italic* or _Emphasis_
+ **Bold** or __Bold__
+ ***Bold italic*** or ___Bold italic__
