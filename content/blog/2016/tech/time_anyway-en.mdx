If you choose an easy life, someone else is surely carrying your burden.

Some things may not succeed even if you try.

But if you do not try, failure is certain.

You leave early and come home late so your daughter, your wife, and your parents can sleep until they wake naturally.

Life has always been a journey of displacement and hardship.

All beings suffer; all forms are empty in essence.

This road is painfully short. The more you dream of it, the more you must travel through wind and rain.

Do right by those who chose the same boat and travel with you.

		By:Time Will Not Change Everything (Wang Yuancheng)
