## Sometimes a cup of tea with someone is enough to know whether they are the person you seek. Everything you do, every movement and every look, is your calling card.
+ Tidy up, go home after work, and make something good: stir-fried pork with green peppers. Now I feel hungry again!
+ Every road you have walked is a kind of wealth.
