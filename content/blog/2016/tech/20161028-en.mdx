## Yesterday they said I got angry
+ I cannot remember hearing that for a long time. I have changed so much this year that I am no longer quite myself. Perhaps I understand now why experience makes people shrewder.
+ Some things need principles and boundaries. The workplace really is full of traps. Be careful.
+ I have always believed in speaking less, listening more, and doing more. But perhaps that is not good either. Looking detached does not stop you from falling into a trap. Life really is damn hard.

## Remembering the times I have lost my temper with you over the years
+ Perhaps it really had reached the point where I could take no more.
+ I can count those occasions on my fingers. Well, there it is.
