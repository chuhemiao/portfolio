The time we truly enjoy life may be only a little over ten years. Life is not smooth: setbacks and difficulties are inevitable. They make us disappointed and depressed, and we feel sorry for ourselves. Everyone handles this differently. Here, join the editor at Huayi Fortune-Telling to look at when the zodiac signs feel most sorry for themselves.
1.Aquarius: during emotional ups and downs
Aquarius people experience strong emotional swings. One moment they feel fine, and the next they feel there is nowhere in the world that belongs to them. They often feel lost and do not know what to do. They are sensitive and easily hurt, with small things causing big emotional changes. They often feel sorry for themselves and realize that everyone is an independent person and no one can be another’s permanent support. At such times, they want to visit an unfamiliar city and escape everyone they know.
