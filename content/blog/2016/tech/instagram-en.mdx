##  `data[i].caption.text==null`error

+ Open the Instagram app and share a photo. Fill in the caption field; otherwise, `data[i].caption.text==null`raises an error, preventing uploaded photos from displaying correctly.

+ To delete a photo, open the photo list in the mobile app, find the uploaded photo, tap the top-right ... menu, and choose Delete.
