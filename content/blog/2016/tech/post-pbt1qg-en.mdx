## My War
+ 1944–1948 My War is an oral history by an author who served in the Nationalist New Sixth Army and 207th Division during the War of Resistance against Japan and the civil war. It records how his happy childhood family was destroyed by Japanese bombardment and how he joined the resistance. At sixteen, he volunteered and, with thousands his age, joined the India–Burma expeditionary force to fight Japan and reopen the Burma Road, the vital international supply route to China. It reveals little-known war details and moving scenes of united resistance. In 1946 the New Sixth Army moved to the northeastern front. Disgusted by civil war, the author left the army three times, organized student protests against recruitment from schools, and fled police and military police. Hardship ultimately forced him back into the army as cannon fodder.
![My War](https://source.shengxuezixun.com/images%2Fmywar.jpg?imageMogr2/thumbnail/250x320)

## Soul Clash
+ Soul Clash (Dota Legend) is an action card mobile game developed by Lilith Games [1] and published by Longtu. Collect chibi heroes and strengthen the team through equipment enchantment, skill upgrades, and hero advancement.
