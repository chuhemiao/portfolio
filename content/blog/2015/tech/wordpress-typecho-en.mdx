## Yesterday I noticed Typecho on someone’s blog and decided on a whim to switch. Here is how I migrated the database.

1.  Step One
    * First, back up your existing database. I will not go into the method here.

2.  Step Two

    * After backing up the database, remove the previous website and download Typecho from http://typecho.org/, or from GitHub: https://github.com/typecho/typecho. Upload the files to your server with FTP. The installation steps are on the official website, so I will skip them here.

3.  Step Three

    * Now the important part: find the WordPress-to-Typecho plugin on the official site (http://docs.typecho.org/plugins/wordpress-to-typecho).

4.  Step Four

    * Upload it to /usr/plugins and open the administration panel.

    * Open Plugin Management from the Console dropdown.

    * Activate the wordpresstotypecho plugin.

    * Click Settings for the wordpresstotypecho plugin.

    * Fill in the database, username, and other information, then save.

    * Select Import Data from WordPress, which appears in the Console dropdown.

    * Complete the import. If it fails, check your database settings and other configuration. You can disable the plugin after importing without affecting other features.

5.  Step Five

    * If the import fails, you can remove unused database tables. On my Alibaba Cloud server, I imported the previous backup into the current tables (note that the table prefixes differ). Then fill in the plugin configuration and run the conversion.
