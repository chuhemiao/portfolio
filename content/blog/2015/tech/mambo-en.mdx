>Last week I bought a wristband on a whim from Gaopeng. After paying, I checked JD.com and found it was cheaper there than in the group buy. What a surprise. It arrived on Monday, and I tried it out. At first I did not know how to use it, but the manual said it needed activation: just connect it to a USB port. The first day showed fewer than 4,000 steps, which seemed reasonable given the distance from Wangjing to the office. The next morning I checked sleep monitoring. I had still been awake at midnight, yet it counted that as light sleep. It felt rather fake. I do not know how it works, and searching Baidu did not explain it. Step counting seems decent, but the other features are disappointing. Another advantage is that it can serve as a watch.
 >>Mengyao Qiyuan — To Life
