>Sometimes losing someone may be a good thing. If they were only passing through your life, why care so much? Do we learn to cherish something only after losing it? Perhaps not. When you begin to feel afraid about a woman, it means the situation has constrained your life and your heart, and you want to escape it. Pressure grows without your noticing. You become afraid of your own actions, afraid she will fall in love with you, and afraid the other woman in your heart will leave. Perhaps this is life.
 >>Mengyao Qiyuan
