## TL;DR

Papertrade is a synthetic perpetuals exchange on Hyperliquid's HyperEVM, offering only BTC and ETH markets with up to 1000x leverage. Users trade against a USDC pool that started at $0, and losing traders receive newly minted $PAPER. In its first 9 days, cumulative volume reached **$1.35T** across **22,869** users. DefiLlama reports cumulative revenue of **$60.79M**, of which **$56.14M** has been distributed to $PAPER stakers. By revenue relative to TVL, it is currently one of the most profitable on-chain protocols.

The model, however, essentially pits traders against the House:

- **85%** of revenue comes from retained user losses: $51.5M of LP Surplus.
- House funds currently amount to only **$3.38M**, against approximately **$99M** of users' net long exposure.
- A simultaneous BTC/ETH rise of approximately 3–4% could exhaust House funds, putting profitable traders' withdrawals into a queue.
- $PAPER currently cannot be transferred and has no market price.
- Revenue is falling rapidly: approximately $9.3M per day over the first 3 days, versus only $2.37M over the latest 24h.

For an ordinary user, this looks more like a casino offering rebates on losses than an airdrop farm with an arbitrage opportunity.

## Mechanics and Team

Papertrade's central design is an exchange with no order book, where users trade against a bootstrapped LP. At both opening and closing, contracts use a precompile to read Hyperliquid's BBO midpoint. Users enter synthetic swaps against the LP; no actual order is placed on Hyperliquid. There is therefore no funding, slippage, or spread, only an OI cap for each asset. [Papertrade Docs](https://docs.papertrade.xyz)

- **Direct LP deposits are not permitted**: the LP grows only through user losses. When it cannot pay, profitable traders receive queued debt, repaid on a first-come, first-served basis using subsequent losses.
- **Fees are asymmetric**: losing traders pay no additional fee. Profitable closes incur an “asymmetric impact” discount on profits: the smaller the price move, the larger the discount. A 2% fee then applies to the remainder. Profitable moves below 0.2bps receive no payout. [airdrops.io](https://airdrops.io/papertrade/)
- **Stakers have two revenue sources**: (1) 1% of settled trade PnL; (2) all LP funds exceeding the **$5M** cap. [Papertrade Docs](https://docs.papertrade.xyz)

The cofounders are two well-known, semi-anonymous traders, **Jez (@izebel_eth)** and **Blurr (@blurr)**. [Bankless](https://www.bankless.com/read/what-to-know-about-papertrade-s-launch)

- Contracts are upgradeable, controlled by a **2-of-3 multisig** comprising izebel_eth, blurr, and UngusTrade, with a **7-day timelock**. [airdrops.io](https://airdrops.io/papertrade/)
- In May 2026, the project announced it had engaged Guardian Audits. [IQ.wiki](https://iq.wiki/wiki/papertrade) Third-party summaries, however, note that the documentation contains no public audit report. This needs independent checking.
- The project has no VC backing or fundraising record.

| Dimension | Situation | Assessment |

| Contract permissions | 2-of-3 multisig, 7-day timelock | Centralized upgrade authority |

## PAPER Token Supply

$PAPER is an ERC-20 token on HyperEVM with a fully fair launch: no premine, team or VC allocation, airdrop, or unlocks. Every token is minted when a user realizes a loss. [Papertrade Docs](https://docs.papertrade.xyz)

| Metric | Value (2026-10-19) |
|---|---:|
| Total supply | **8,677,225,186** |
| Staked | **8,612,435,206 (99.25%)** |
| Cumulative staking rewards | **$54.06M** USDC |
| Cumulative distribution per token | Approximately **$0.0062** |

The minting curve works as follows: when the LP is below $2M, each $1 lost mints 100 PAPER. Above that level, the rate declines according to `100 × (120M / (120M + H))²`, where H is the pool's historical peak earnings and can only increase. On 10-17, H was approximately $222.7M, implying approximately **12.3 tokens** minted per $1 lost. The acquisition cost per PAPER had thus risen from approximately 1 cent at launch to approximately **8 cents**. [airdrops.io](https://airdrops.io/papertrade/)

Important concerns:

- Early tokens acquired for 1 cent share the same reward pool as tokens now acquired for approximately 8 cents, substantially diluting returns for later entrants.
- Cumulative distributions per token are currently approximately 0.6 cents, below the current acquisition cost.
- If the LP falls below $2M, issuance returns to 100 tokens per $1, creating another round of dilution.

⚠️ **Imitation-token warning**: $PAPER currently cannot be transferred and has no official DEX pair. A token named PAPER exists on Solana with $0 in 24h volume; Coinbase also has a “papertradexyz” page labeled as Solana. Neither is the official token. Do not buy them.

## Risk Incident and Main Risks

**Oracle manipulation has already occurred.** On launch day, 10-10, wallet `0x2025...840e` traded ETH in size on Hyperliquid, moving its BBO price by approximately 0.12%. It used 1000x leverage on Papertrade to earn approximately **$1.28M** on $3.18B in volume, while paying only approximately $52K in Hyperliquid fees. The team subsequently tightened OI limits on-chain and at the relay layer. [The Block via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/the_block:995b84c64094b:0-trader-nets-1-28-million-on-new-1-000x-exchange-papertrade-by-moving-hyperliquid-s-eth-prices/) DefiLlama records this as an Oracle Manipulation security incident.

| Risk | Severity | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Queued payouts | High | House funds of $3.38M against approximately $99M net exposure; a one-way market can trigger queued debt, with no fixed payout time |
| Oracle manipulation | High | Large trades can move the BBO midpoint; the official risk page acknowledges this |
| No PAPER liquidity | High | Nontransferable, no price, and no scheduled date for enabling transfers |
| Dilution from hedged token farming | Medium | Delphi's tempest notes that long/short hedging can mint PAPER at low cost [Bankless](https://www.bankless.com/read/what-to-know-about-papertrade-s-launch) |
| Falling revenue | Medium | Daily revenue fell from approximately $9.3M to $2.37M |
| Centralization and infrastructure | Medium | A multisig can upgrade contracts; dependence on HyperEVM blockspace, with the Dashboard showing a 3M gas budget per block and 9.62M queued |
| Extreme leverage | High | At 1000x leverage, an adverse move of approximately 0.052% triggers full liquidation |

## Conclusion

Papertrade's mechanism is genuinely novel. It passes 100% of House profits to token holders; the team receives only 50% of trading and liquidation fees, approximately 7.6% of total revenue. Within 9 days, it generated $60M in revenue and $1.35T in volume. Yet its revenue comes almost entirely from retail losses, without a sustainable fee-based moat. House funds and user exposure are severely mismatched, and oracle manipulation has already occurred. Revenue is falling rapidly, while retaining users depends on a continuing inflow of new loss-bearing funds.

**What to watch**: Focus on four indicators:

1. Whether House funds can remain above $5M, determining whether stakers can resume receiving overflow distributions.
2. Whether queued debt appears.
3. When PAPER transfers are enabled, and whether its first market price exceeds the approximately 8-cent acquisition cost.
4. Whether daily revenue can stabilize above $2M.

If you already trade BTC/ETH with high leverage, PAPER can be treated as a rebate on losses; remember to stake it. Entering solely to farm PAPER, however, means exchanging real losses for a token without a market, whose distributions depend on the next group of losing traders. I do not recommend doing so.
