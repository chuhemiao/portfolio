Concrete ($CT)’s **TGE and initial spot-market launches were concentrated on September 30, 2026**. The earliest record is **Binance Alpha at 08:00 UTC**, followed by spot trading on Coinbase, Bitget, Gate, OKX, and others at **around 10:00 UTC**. Additional perpetual markets followed on **October 1**, before listings on Upbit and Bithumb on **October 19** expanded its presence in the Korean market.

## What This Timeline Means

**First, initial liquidity was relatively concentrated.**  
$CT entered multiple spot markets on September 30, so early price discovery would be concentrated among several major exchanges. Differences in pair depth, regional user composition, and market-making arrangements may create short-lived price gaps.

**Second, derivatives arrived very quickly.**  
Perpetuals on OKX, Aster, Bybit, Bitget, Gate, and Binance followed rapidly after the TGE. Early-listing analysis therefore needs to focus on funding rates, open interest, and liquidation volume alongside spot trading volume.

**Third, the Korean market was a second-stage expansion.**  
Upbit and Bithumb listed on October 19, approximately **19 days** after the initial launch. Upbit offered CT/BTC, CT/KRW, and CT/USDT, while Bithumb offered CT/KRW. This may increase local liquidity in the Korean market, but it may also bring regional premiums or discounts.

**Data definitions.**  
The times above come from the supplied exchange-listing event records, using `effective_at` as the effective time. Multiple trading pairs on the same exchange with the same effective time are grouped together. The original Aster records contain duplicate or near-duplicate entries, so only one entry is retained at the exchange-and-product level. XT’s time instead follows the specific trading time in its official announcement. An exchange “listing record” does not establish that trading is available in every region as of the report date. Actual availability still depends on account location, product status, and exchange rules.

## Conclusion

Concrete ($CT)’s main launch date was **2026-09-30**: Binance Alpha came first, followed by spot trading on several major exchanges. Derivatives coverage expanded on **2026-10-01**, followed by entry into Upbit and Bithumb on **2026-10-19**.

**Conclusion.** To study $CT’s initial price performance, treat **September 30, 08:00–10:00 UTC** as the first price-discovery window, **the October 1 perpetual listings** as the leverage-volatility window, and **the October 19 Korean exchange listings** as a second regional-liquidity event.
