## 7. Check the number of active iPhone lines

An iPhone can store multiple eSIMs, but only a limited number of lines can be active simultaneously.

For example, the phone may already store:

- A Chinese mobile number;
- A Japanese number;
- A travel eSIM;
- A Saily eSIM.

If Saily is installed but has no network, or the line shows `Off`, check as follows:

1. Open Settings → Cellular.
2. Turn off a SIM or eSIM that is not currently needed.
3. Turn on the Saily eSIM.

This does not necessarily indicate installation failure. The device’s active-line limit may simply have been reached.
