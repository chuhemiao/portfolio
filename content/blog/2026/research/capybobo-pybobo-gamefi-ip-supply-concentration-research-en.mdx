## TL;DR

**$PYBOBO is a funded GameFi/collectible-toy project with an IP narrative and centralized-exchange coverage. For now, however, it looks more like a volatile, event-driven asset than a long-term allocation with validated fundamentals.**

At **2026-10-21 14:00 UTC**, $PYBOBO traded at approximately **$0.000663–$0.000666**, with around **$23.5M** in market cap, **$66.6M** FDV, and **$4.27M** in 24-hour volume. Short-term figures were weak: **-14.6% over 7 days** and **-5.75% over 24 hours**, although the 30-day change remained **+20.7%**.

The most important risks concern more than valuation:

1. Market data and the website show a clear supply conflict: market data uses **100B PYBOBO**, while the current website states **10B PYBOBO + 1B CROCO**.
2. Solana holdings are highly concentrated: the top 10 addresses hold approximately **72.4%**, and the top 20 approximately **97.9%** of total supply. These may include treasury, lockup, and airdrop-distribution accounts, but sufficiently detailed labels are not available.
3. The product roadmap is imaginative, but active users, revenue, physical sales, and redemption data have not yet adequately demonstrated token value capture.

**Assessment: A high-risk watchlist asset.** Until token migration, float, unlock schedules, and product KPIs are clarified, it is unsuitable as a high-conviction long-term long position.

## Tokenomics: The Largest Data-Quality Risk

At least three definitions currently coexist:

| Data basis | PYBOBO supply | Role | Main issue |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Market/project data | **100B** | Currently traded token | Consistent with float, market cap, and FDV |
| Current website | **10B** | PYBOBO governance “Star” | Also introduces a 1B CROCO utility token |
| Earlier project information | **100B** | Game rewards, ecosystem, and community token | Inconsistent with the website's new two-token narrative |

The current website describes $PYBOBO as governance and $CROCO as a utility token for the game and economy. Current project materials, however, do not also confirm a specific $CROCO contract and migration plan. The website lists this $PYBOBO allocation:

- Community: 50%
- Treasury: 20%
- Team: 13%
- Partners: 12%
- Liquidity: 5%

These percentages broadly match earlier 100B-version materials. The issue may stem from a website update, token migration, or version change, but evidence is insufficient to determine which.

Using 100B supply:

- Approximately **35.33%** circulates;
- Approximately **64.67B** remains noncirculating;
- FDV is approximately **2.83 times** market cap.

The market therefore cannot look only at the current 23.5M market cap; future supply releases also matter.

Third-party news has also reported multiple unlocks in 2026. Some reports, however, show inconsistent units, with millions of tokens assigned millions of dollars in value, which cannot be reconciled directly with the current price and 100B supply. Their figures cannot be treated as reliable amounts for the next unlock. Structured unlock data likewise did not return a verifiable complete calendar within the original research window. **This does not mean there are no unlocks; it means the precise schedule and quantities cannot currently be confirmed.**

**Conclusion**: Until supply, the two-token model, and unlock schedules are clarified, $PYBOBO's FDV is not a reliable valuation anchor.

## Conclusion

Capybobo's strengths are **real funding, a strong IP direction, combining games with physical collectibles, and expanding exchange coverage**. It nevertheless remains in transition from narrative and distribution toward product and revenue validation.

The central tension is that the market already gives $PYBOBO some liquidity and valuation, while supply definitions, the two-token model, actual float, and product KPIs remain insufficiently transparent. Its sharp rise and retreat around Bithumb listing expectations also indicate a market more inclined to trade events than long-term fundamentals.

**Final assessment**: Treat $PYBOBO as **a high-risk, event-driven speculative watchlist asset**, not a certain long-term allocation. The long-term case improves materially only when supply and unlock disclosures are reconciled, the top 20 addresses are explained, and verifiable growth appears in game activity and physical sales.
