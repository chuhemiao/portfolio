## TL;DR

**In this channel the five Mag7 instruments are ranked the exact reverse of public markets: TSLAB alone is larger than the other four combined. But most of this channel's real Mag7 exposure is not in these five.**

- **TSLAB's market cap of `$23.68M` is `12.7%` more than AAPLB (`$5.45M`) plus MSFTB (`$4.37M`) plus AMZNB (`$3.73M`) plus METAB (`$7.46M`) combined.** It is `53.0%` of the group, `5.42x` MSFTB and `4.35x` AAPLB.
- **The book's 30-day median across 100 instruments is `−16.71%` with 82 negative — and all five here beat the median, three outright positive.**
- **Of the 18 instruments on the book that are up, three of the four largest by market cap are in this group**: TSLAB `$23.68M` (1st), AAPLB `$5.45M` (2nd), MSFTB `$4.37M` (4th). The other 14 positive instruments are all under `$2.50M`.
- **But `76.9%` of this channel's Mag7 exposure is not in these five.** The seven Mag7 names on the book come to `$193.49M`, and GOOGLB `$104.99M` (3rd on the book) plus NVDAB `$43.80M` (8th) are `76.9%` of it. Neither is in this group.
- **QQQB alone is `2.20x` all five combined.** At `$98.22M` it ranks 5th on the book and listed the same day as MSFTB and METAB (2026-07-02). **So "this channel does not want Apple" is wrong — it just does not want it one name at a time.**
- **Counter-example: TSLAB is the smallest instrument in its own listing cohort.** Six listed on 2026-06-11 and TSLAB ranks 6th of 6, while NVDAB from the same day is `1.85x` its size.
- **Market cap and turnover run backwards inside the group.** The largest, TSLAB, turns `0.548x` (67th on the book); the smallest, AMZNB, turns `1.617x` (26th). Drop TSLAB and group turnover rises from `0.7812x` to `1.0437x`.

## 6. Structure

All of this comes from this site's issuer record, the same data behind [/stocks/bstocks](/en/stocks/bstocks):

- **Legal wrapper**: tracker certificate, ADGM / FSRA listed certificates, issuing entity BTech Holdings Limited. The holder receives a certificate tracking the underlying price, not a share registration.
- **Voting rights**: none.
- **Dividends**: passed through, but after 30% US withholding, then reinvested via a multiplier.
- **Redemption**: supported, KYC required, `$5` minimum.
- **Proof of reserve**: attestation, not live on-chain proof.
- **US persons**: blocked.
- **Chain**: BNB Chain only. BSC deposits only; sending on the wrong network loses the assets.
- **Issuance model**: exchange self-issued; the only venue is Binance, operated by the issuer.
- **Grade on this site**: B.

Two of those have concrete meaning for this group.

**Voting rights are none, and shareholder votes at these five companies carry real content in public markets.** Tesla compensation packages, Meta dual-class share structure, Apple and Amazon shareholder proposals are all matters genuinely voted on each year in public markets. **What the token holder receives is price exposure, without that layer.** This is not a defect peculiar to this channel — it is the definition of the tracker-certificate wrapper — but in the Mag7 group what is given up is more concrete than it would be in a small-cap name.

**Dividends are "30% withholding, then reinvested via a multiplier," and some of these five pay.** Apple and Microsoft pay quarterly in public markets. Passing a dividend through, then withholding 30%, then reinvesting at a multiplier this piece does not know, means **the token's unit price path and the underlying share's unit path are not the same path** — one of the reasons section 8 gives no premium figure.

## 7. This snapshot was taken on a Saturday

The timestamp `2026-10-31T06:06:52Z` is Saturday morning UTC. The US cash market closed Friday at 20:00 UTC and does not reopen until Monday — **for more than two days after this snapshot, these token prices are the only prices these five names have.**

This group deserves the reminder especially, because **its range positions cluster near the top**: AAPLB `78.1%`, MSFTB `80.2%`, TSLAB `76.4%`, sitting only `−3.5%`, `−6.9%` and `−7.6%` from their highs respectively. **"Only 3.5% below its all-time high" is a sentence with no cash-market quote behind it when you read it on a weekend.**

The same issuer has already provided a quantified version: [one stock, four books](/en/blog/aapl-four-books-tokenized-equity-liquidity-en) laid AAPL's four tokenized versions side by side on a single Saturday and concluded that **a quote during non-trading hours is a market maker's opinion, not a market price**. That piece and this one share AAPLB as an instrument and overlap in window, so its conclusion applies directly to AAPLB's `+3.20%` in section 3.

The 24-hour changes in this piece (AMZNB `+5.82%`, TSLAB `+1.25%`) cover Friday 06:06 UTC to Saturday 06:06 UTC, **which includes a full Friday US trading session**, so they are not purely closed-market quotes.

## 8. What this method cannot compute

**First, it cannot compute premium or discount.** The snapshot carries only the token's own price, with no reference price for the underlying stock. More fundamentally, the bStocks dividend mechanism is "30% US withholding, then reinvested via a multiplier" — **without knowing the current multiplier, the token price and the underlying share price are not comparable in the first place**. So no premium figure appears anywhere in this piece, and `$345.04` cannot be read alongside any Apple quote.

**Second, it cannot compute shares per token.** The snapshot has no conversion ratio. TSLAB has 59,212 tokens circulating at `$399.97` and AAPLB has 15,781 at `$345.04` — without the ratio those numbers cannot be converted into any share count, nor used to compare how much underlying asset each instrument represents. **Every comparison in section 2 is a comparison between token floats.**

**Third, it cannot compute index constituents.** Section 4 uses QQQB and SPYB to cut down this group's claim, but **the snapshot has no index constituent or weight field**. This piece says only that QQQ and SPY are broad index products and that their token float is far larger than any single name's; **it does not and cannot quantify how much of those two ETFs is exposure to these five names.** That layer requires the funds' own published holdings.

**Fourth, the range-width windows are unequal.** The five are 142 / 121 / 95 / 121 / 95 days, and **AAPLB and AMZNB at 95 days do not enter the comparison across the 46 instruments live 100 days or more.** So section 5's width ranking contains only TSLAB, METAB and MSFTB; AAPLB's `1.189x` is comparable only inside its own 95-day cohort (the table in section 4) and cannot be set directly against SPYB's `1.074x`.

**Fifth, market cap is the CMC measure** (price times circulating supply), and the book total of `$1,318.38M` is the sum of these 100 rows; the bStocks line on [this site's /stocks page](/en/stocks) shows an on-chain size of `$914M` — **two numbers from different sources at different moments that will not tie.** This piece uses the former throughout.

**Sixth, 100 of 105.** CMC reports 105 instruments and the snapshot in hand holds 100; the missing 5 appear in no statistic here. **So the accurate phrasing of "the seven Mag7 names" is "the seven Mag7 names among these 100 rows"** — if any of the missing 5 is relevant, the 76.9% in section 4 changes.

**Seventh, turnover is 24h volume divided by market cap.** A small denominator inflates the ratio. These five span `$3.73M` to `$23.68M` in market cap, a factor of 6.3, **so part of the "market cap and turnover run backwards" result in section 2 is a denominator effect** and must not be read entirely as a difference in trading interest. AMZNB's `1.617x` sits on `$6.04M` of absolute volume against TSLAB's `0.548x` on `$12.99M` — **in absolute volume TSLAB is still the largest in the group.**

**Eighth, one 30-day window is not a conclusion.** The "three of them rose" in section 3 is the result of roughly 2026-10-01 to 10-31. The 30-day spread across these five runs `+11.78%` to `−16.00%`, a gap of `27.8` percentage points — **five names that all belong to the Mag7 separated by nearly 28 points in a single month, which by itself says no common Mag7 factor was operating in this window.**

**Conclusion.** In this channel the five Mag7 instruments are ranked the reverse of public markets: **TSLAB at `$23.68M` is `12.7%` larger than AAPLB plus MSFTB plus AMZNB plus METAB combined, and `5.42x` MSFTB.** And in a month when 82 instruments fell and the median was `−16.71%`, **all five beat the median, three are positive, and three of the four largest of the book's 18 positive instruments are in this group** — the other 14 are all under `$2.50M`. That is the one conclusion here that holds up on size.

But the argument has to stop at the boundary of the five members. **`76.9%` of this channel's Mag7 exposure sits in GOOGLB (`$104.99M`, 3rd on the book) and NVDAB (`$43.80M`, 8th), neither of which is in this group, and GOOGLB by itself is `135%` more than all five combined; while QQQB alone at `$98.22M` is `2.20x` the five.** So "this channel does not want Apple" is wrong — **it just does not want it one name at a time.**

Two counter-examples narrow the claim further. **TSLAB ranks last on market cap among the six instruments in its own listing cohort**, with NVDAB from the same day `1.85x` its size, so "TSLAB is large" is only a within-group statement. **AAPLB is not the largest in its own ten-instrument cohort either** — AMATB is `65.7%` larger, and AMATB's turnover is the lowest of all 100 on the book, which shows that token float is a coarse proxy for preference to begin with. What does survive for AAPLB is path: **of the ten in its cohort it has the narrowest range (`1.189x`), the highest position in range (`78.1%`) and the smallest distance from its high (`−3.5%`).**

One last thing to read together with all of it: **the Mag7 is the calmest stretch of path on this book.** The 15 narrowest range widths among the 46 instruments live 100 days or more contain every one of the five Mag7 names that reach that window, and the two narrowest of all are SPYB and QQQB. **What this channel holds most of is also what travels flattest.**
