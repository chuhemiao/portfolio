## TL;DR

**This looks like the most resilient group on the book, and half of that resilience is a listing date - three of its four gainers have a "30-day" window shorter than 30 days.**

- **Only 18 of the 100 instruments are up over 30 days, and four of them are in this group of five**: LLYB `+21.31%` (3rd on the book), MRNAB `+15.34%` (6th), WENB `+12.62%` (7th), BRKBB `+1.08%` (16th).
- **The group median distance below the post-listing high is `−2.3%`, the shallowest of all 20 groups**, against `−7.6%` for the next one (the cold half of the mag7) and a book median of `−27.1%`.
- **But LLYB has been listed 24 days, WENB 32 and BRKBB 12.** Of the 20 instruments on the book listed under 33 days, **9 are positive (45%)**, against **9 of the other 80 (11.3%)** - **a factor of 4.**
- **Control for the window and this group is down to MRNAB** (60 days, `+15.34%`) and NFLXB (88 days, `−11.05%`), the only two with complete windows.
- **Being newly listed is not the same as being up, either.** Of the five instruments that listed on the same day as BRKBB, three are down between 9.88% and 14.78% over the identical 12 days; of the four in the LLYB cohort, two fell 38.92% and 39.32% over the identical 24 days.
- **The group aggregate turnover of `3.9840x` is the highest of all 20 groups, and it is one instrument.** Excluding MRNAB it is `0.8472x`, essentially the book member median of `0.867x` - MRNAB alone is **85.8%** of the group volume.
- **This snapshot cannot produce a premium.** There is no underlying reference price and no dividend reinvestment multiplier, and this is the group where that limit bites hardest.

**So being up over 30 days is four times as common among newly listed instruments as among the rest.** The reason is not mysterious: an instrument listed 12 days ago can have at most 12 days in its "30-day" field, **and it did not exist for most of the drawdown that hit the book.** This is the same limit as the fourth point of section 8; here it determines the entire argument.

**Control for the window and only two of the five are comparable**: MRNAB (60 days, `+15.34%`) and NFLXB (88 days, `−11.05%`). **In other words, on instruments with complete windows, the claim that pharma and consumer held up rests on one positive case and one negative one.**

## 4. Counter-example: listing late is not the same as going up

**The explanation above has an obvious hole: if being newly listed were enough to dodge the drawdown, every new instrument would be positive.** They are not.

|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| **BRKBB** | 2026-10-19 | 12 | **+1.08%** | common stock |
| TMFB | 2026-10-19 | 12 | +4.81% | 3x leveraged ETF |
| VSTB | 2026-10-19 | 12 | **−9.88%** | common stock |
| BOTB | 2026-10-19 | 12 | **−11.58%** | unknown |
| KSTRB | 2026-10-19 | 12 | **−14.78%** | ETF |
| **LLYB** | 2026-10-07 | 24 | **+21.31%** | common stock |
| JPMB | 2026-10-07 | 24 | +2.89% | common stock |
| SECZB | 2026-10-07 | 24 | **−38.92%** | unknown |
| USDEB | 2026-10-07 | 24 | **−39.32%** | unknown |

**Of the five instruments that listed on the same day as BRKBB, three are down between 9.88% and 14.78% over the identical 12 days.** Of the four in the LLYB cohort, two fell close to 40% over the identical 24 days - **more than twice the book median of `−16.71%`.** Across the 20 instruments listed under 33 days, the 30-day median is `−4.94%`, **still negative.**

**So a short window is not a shield; it only shrinks the sample of the fall that gets measured.** The factor of 4 in section 3 is real, but what it establishes is that "a higher share of new instruments is positive", not that "new instruments are positive". **Only the first of those supports the argument here, and the fact that three of this group's four gainers happen to land on the new-instrument side still needs an explanation - one this snapshot cannot give.**

**The second counter-example is NFLXB, the only loser in the group.** Its position in range of 3.0% is the 6th lowest on the book (above only INTCB, DJTB, INTWB, CBRSB and SECZB), its price of `$62.20` sits almost exactly on its post-listing low of `$61.54`, and it is `−25.4%` from its high. It reads as the bad apple of the group.

**Put it back among the 10 instruments that listed on the same day and it is the best one**:

```text

CRDOB  -24.66%   BMNRB  -25.07%   COHRB  -27.97%
```

**The only loser in this group ranks 1st in its own cohort.** Which means all five members of this group are the best or close to the best of their respective cohorts - **and that cannot be seen in the 30-day column, because that column puts an 88-day instrument and a 12-day instrument side by side.**

**A third counter-example: going up is not a property of these underlyings either.** ADBEB (`+4.39%`, software) and PDDB (`+0.57%`, an ADR) at the same 32 days, JPMB (`+2.89%`, a bank) at 24 days and BBB (`+0.02%`) at 19 days are all positive, and none of them has anything to do with pharma, fast food or insurance. **And the top two of the book's 18 gainers are −3x inverse ETFs** (SOXSB `+70.82%`, SQQQB `+24.94%`) - a product that shorts a falling market goes up by construction, which is a different question from which sector held up.

**Only two names in the turnover top 15 are up over 30 days: MRNAB and UVXYB.** And UVXYB is a 2x long-volatility product - in a month with a book median of `−16.71%` it rises mechanically. **MRNAB is the only directionally long common stock in the turnover top 15 that is up over 30 days.**

Absolute volume is just as anomalous:

- **MRNAB trades `$23.66M` in a day, 13th on the book, while its market cap ranks 53rd - a gap of 40 places.**
- **It is 2.71% of book volume on 0.17% of book market cap, a ratio of 15.6.**
- **Its volume is 6.0 times the other four members combined (`$3.92M`)**, while its market cap is only slightly below that of NFLXB.

On the range side: the MRNAB width of `1.934x` (post-listing high `$254.19`, low `$131.43`, price `$220.80`) is **the widest of its own 4-name cohort** (STXB `1.615x`, CRWDB `1.422x`, SQQQB `1.368x`), while its position in range of 72.8% is the third highest in this group. **So it produced the largest swing in this group, closed high in that swing, and is the 13th largest volume on the book - and it is the only instrument on the book where all three are true at once.**

**But this snapshot cannot answer why.** The question left open by group 2 (the group definition here says the cause of that gap has not been established) is still unresolved in this data, for three reasons, all of which have to be stated:

- **The 24-hour volume is an aggregate with no trade count and no order book.** That `$23.66M` could be one large print or sustained liquidity, and this snapshot cannot tell the difference.
- **The test group 2 used - compare against the spot sibling token - cannot be run here**, because there is no second Moderna-linked token on this book, so the leverage factor and the instrument factor cannot be separated.
- **There is no underlying volume.** So the most natural check, whether token-side volume is anomalous relative to the underlying, is not available.

What can be stated is where it sits on this book, not what causes it.

## 6. Structure: what these five tokens actually are

All of this comes from the issuer record on this site rather than being invented here:

- **The wrapper is a tracker certificate**, issued by BTech Holdings Limited as formally listed certificates under ADGM / FSRA - **not equity**.
- **No voting rights.**
- **Dividends are pass-through: 30% US withholding first, then reinvested via a multiplier.**
- **Reserves are covered by attestation, not on-chain proof of reserve.** For comparison, xStocks uses Chainlink proof of reserve, refreshed daily or when reserves move more than 10%, verifiable on chain; an attestation is not.
- **Redemption is available, KYC required, $5 minimum.** (The xStocks primary redemption minimum is $5,000.)
- **US persons are blocked.**
- **A single chain in BNB Chain, traded only on Binance, deposits supported only over BSC - sending over the wrong network loses the asset.**
- **The issuance model is exchange-self**: the issuing entity and the sole trading venue are the same party.

**The dividend line matters more for this group than for the others.** These underlyings include pharma and consumer companies, **and for any of them that pays a dividend the multiplier has already moved since listing** - and this snapshot does not hold the multiplier. So the first point of section 8 is not boilerplate here: **it means part of the divergence between these token prices and their underlying share prices may be reinvestment, and this data cannot separate the two.** Every "−1.5% from the high" and "92.5% of range" in section 2 is a position on the token price series, which is not the same as a position on the underlying.

**The book-narrowest range on BRKBB has to be read the way group 1 established.** [The flagship pricing anchors](/en/blog/bstocks-flagship-pricing-anchors-en) piece already states it: **a narrow range on a newly listed instrument carries no information** - BRKBB has been listed 12 days, and `1.031x` only says it has not moved much in 12 days, not that it is stable.

What this report can add is two other numbers: **the BRKBB 24-hour volume of `$125.3K` is the 4th lowest on the book** (below it are TMFB `$91.6K`, CRMB `$91.8K` and VSTB `$105.3K`), **and its turnover of `0.223x` ranks 93rd.** Which is to say **the narrowest range and the closest-to-high name on the whole book belongs to a token that has barely traded.**

**The same property holds book-wide.** Of the six instruments closest to their own highs, three are in this group, two in broad market and equipment, and one in financials and payments:

```text
BRKBB  -1.5%  12d     LLYB  -1.6%  24d     TMFB  -1.9%  12d
JPMB   -2.1%  24d     WENB  -2.3%  32d     SPYB  -3.1%  117d
```

**Five of the six have been listed under 33 days**, the one exception being SPYB (117 days, an S&P 500 ETF, which its own report explains separately).

## 7. This snapshot was taken on a Saturday

The timestamp `2026-10-31T06:06:52Z` is a Saturday morning UTC. The US cash market closed Friday at 20:00 UTC and does not reopen until Monday.

This has already been quantified once on this issuer: [one stock, four books](/en/blog/aapl-four-books-tokenized-equity-liquidity-en) laid the four tokenized versions of AAPL side by side on a Saturday and concluded that **a quote outside trading hours is a market maker opinion, not a market price**.

So the 24-hour changes here read as follows: that window (Friday 06:06 UTC to Saturday 06:06 UTC) **contains a full Friday US session**, so it is not a pure closed-market quote; but the snapshot itself lands inside the closed window.

**For the conclusion in section 5, this has a concrete effect.** The MRNAB 24-hour change is `+0.06%`, essentially flat, **while it traded `$23.66M` inside that window** - a flat price on the 13th largest volume on the book is harder to explain under closed-market quoting, because a market maker quote does not have to move with volume in the first place. **That is the concrete consequence of the first of the three limits above: with no trade count and no order book, heavy two-sided volume and a pinned quote cannot be told apart here.**

## 8. What this method cannot compute

**First, no premium or discount.** The snapshot holds only the token price, **not an underlying reference price**. More importantly, the dividend mechanism is "30% US withholding first, then reinvested via a multiplier" - **without knowing the current multiplier, the token price and the share price are not comparable to begin with.** This limit is heaviest on this group, for the reason in section 6. So this report gives no premium figure at all.

**Second, how many shares one token represents cannot be computed.** The snapshot holds no conversion ratio, so circulating supply cannot be converted into any share count. The difference between the LLYB unit price of `$1,416.51` and the WENB price of `$7.17` **cannot be read as one LLYB representing more shares.**

**Third, the range-width windows are not equal length.** The five here are 88 / 60 / 32 / 24 / 12 days - **not one of them qualifies for the 46-name "listed 100 days or more" list**, so no width in this group can be compared against mature instruments (the common-stock width median among those 46 is `1.768x`, and the widest here, MRNAB at `1.934x`, looks close to it on only 60 days). The `1.031x` on BRKBB is the narrowest on the book, and that is a 12-day result.

**Fourth, for an instrument listed under 30 days, its "30-day" field covers less than 30 days.** On the book 15 instruments have been listed under 30 days and 11 under 20, **and two of them are in this group (LLYB at 24, BRKBB at 12), while the 32 days on WENB barely clears it.** In this report that limit is not a footnote - **it is the whole of section 3**, and the reason this report does not treat "this group held up" as a conclusion. The book 30-day median of `−16.71%` cited above, and every rank, contain those 15 clipped values.

**Fifth, market cap is the CMC definition**: price times circulating supply. The `$1,318.38M` book total above is the sum of these 100 rows, while the bStocks row on [the /stocks page of this site](/en/stocks) shows an on-chain size of `$914M` - **two numbers from different sources at different moments, which will not reconcile.**

**Sixth, 100 of 105.** CMC reports 105 instruments, the snapshot in hand holds 100, and the missing 5 appear in no statistic here.

**Seventh, the turnover in section 5 cannot be attributed to any cause.** The 24-hour volume is an aggregate with no trade count, no order book and no underlying volume, and this book holds no second Moderna-linked token to compare against. **So this report states where MRNAB sits on this book and not why it sits there.**

---

**Conclusion.** This group genuinely is the most resilient on this book - four of the 18 gainers, a median distance below the high of `−2.3%` that is the shallowest of all 20 groups, and the book firsts on closest-to-high (BRKBB), highest position in range (LLYB) and best 7 days (WENB) all sitting here.

**But half of that is a listing date.** LLYB has been listed 24 days, WENB 32 and BRKBB 12, and across the book 45% of the 20 instruments listed under 33 days are positive against 11.3% of the other 80 - **a factor of 4.** Control for the window and only MRNAB (60 days, `+15.34%`) and NFLXB (88 days, `−11.05%`) remain comparable: **one positive case and one negative one.**

**And a short window is not a shield**: three of the other instruments that listed on the same day as BRKBB fell between 9.88% and 14.78% over the identical 12 days, and two in the LLYB cohort fell close to 40% over the identical 24. So the factor of 4 in section 3 establishes that a higher share of new instruments is positive, not that new instruments are positive - **and the fact that three of this group's four gainers happen to land on that side is something this snapshot cannot explain.**

**NFLXB, the group's only loser, proves the same point from the other direction**: it sits on its own post-listing low (3.0% of range, 6th lowest on the book) and is nevertheless the best 30-day figure among the 10 instruments that listed with it. **It is not the exception in this group; it is the only member whose window is long enough to have the drawdown fully counted against it.**

The last item is MRNAB. **Its turnover of `10.307x` is 2nd on the book, its volume of `$23.66M` is 13th while its market cap ranks only 53rd, and it is the only directionally long common stock in the turnover top 15 that is up over 30 days.** That position is solid; **its cause is not something this snapshot can supply** - no trade count, no order book, no underlying volume, and no second token on this book tracking the same company to compare against.
